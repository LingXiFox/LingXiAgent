Rules frozen with this manifest:
  - structSize then abiMajor lead every boundary struct; new fields append.
  - Statuses are scalar-only and cross as an out-parameter; calls return int32_t.
  - Reply and describe buffers are allocated by the callee and freed by the caller through the
    table's free function.
  - A major mismatch is a named hard failure; there is no compatibility path.
